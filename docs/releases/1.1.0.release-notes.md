## Notch Lyrics 1.1.0

我们优化了使用体验，也修复了一些 bug，但不慎误食了一锅冒菜。

冒菜已被妥善消化，未影响本次更新的质量。以下是一些真正发生的事：

### 🚑 能打开了

1.0.0 发布时打不开——hardened runtime 加上 ad-hoc 签名，`dyld` 直接把内嵌的
媒体 framework 拒之门外，进程在画出窗口之前就咽了气。所以这一版最大的新功能是：
**能打开**。一个 entitlement 修好它，来龙去脉见[完整说明](docs/releases/1.1.0.md)。

### 🌍 不再开口就是中文

405 条文案全部收进 String Catalog：西班牙语、简体中文完整覆盖，另外十三种
语言覆盖不到一半、其余回退英文。设置 › 通用 里可以直接换语言——改完要重启
一次，这个锅归 macOS，它启动时就定了读哪个语言包，改不了。

### 🧱 设置页减重

十二个设置页统一到同一套组件上。顺手从代码库里请走了一批不干活的代码和
几个没人用的图标，净减约 900 行。它们走得很体面，我们没有送。

### 🔗 分享正在播放

刘海里多了个分享按钮：文字摘要、专辑封面，以及播放器给得出时的歌曲链接。
Spotify 给精确曲目链接；Apple Music 退而求其次给搜索链接，因为 MusicKit 的
`SystemMusicPlayer` 在 macOS 上不给用——这事不赖我们。

### ⚠️ 还是那几句丑话

- 没有 Apple 开发者证书：ad-hoc 签名 + Sparkle EdDSA 验签。首次打开可能需要
  右键 → 打开。
- 下载后先拖进 `/Applications` 再开，否则 App Translocation 会悄悄弄坏
  开机自启和自动更新。
- 自动更新要走 GitHub。连不上的网络收不到更新，而且 Sparkle 失败时
  一声不吭——它管这叫设计。
- 歌词靠 LRCLIB。冷门歌真没有就是没有，我们宁可说没有，也不给你错的。

## Notch Lyrics 1.1.0 (English)

We polished the experience and fixed some bugs. We also accidentally ate a pot
of maocai (Sichuan hotpot-for-one).

The maocai has been fully digested and did not affect the quality of this
release. Things that actually happened:

### 🚑 It opens now

1.0.0 shipped unable to open — hardened runtime plus an ad-hoc signature meant
`dyld` refused to load the embedded media framework, and the process died
before drawing a window. The headline feature of 1.1.0: **it opens.** One
entitlement fixes it; the full story is in the
[release notes](docs/releases/1.1.0.md).

### 🌍 No longer speaks Chinese uninvited

All 405 strings live in a String Catalog. Spanish and Simplified Chinese are
complete; thirteen more languages cover just under half and fall back to
English. Pick your language in Settings › General — it asks for a restart,
because macOS decides which language bundle to load at process start. Its
rules, not ours.

### 🧱 Settings on a diet

All twelve settings pages were rebuilt on one shared component set. We also
showed a batch of dead code and a few unused icons the door — about 900 lines
net. They left with dignity; nobody cried.

### 🔗 Share what's playing

A share button in the notch: text summary, album art, and a track link when
the player offers one. Spotify resolves an exact track URL; Apple Music falls
back to a search link, because MusicKit's `SystemMusicPlayer` is not available
on macOS — that part is not on us.

### ⚠️ The usual fine print

- No Apple developer certificate: ad-hoc signed, verified by Sparkle's EdDSA.
  First launch may need right-click → Open.
- Drag the app to `/Applications` before launching, or App Translocation
  quietly breaks launch-at-login and automatic updates.
- Updates come from GitHub. Unreachable networks get silence, because Sparkle
  fails silently by design.
- Lyrics depend on LRCLIB. Obscure tracks may genuinely have none — we would
  rather say so than show you a wrong match.
